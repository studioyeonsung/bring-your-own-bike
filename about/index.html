ABOUT BRING YOUR OWN BIKE

BYOB(Bring Your Own Bike) is a participatory performance series that explores polluted weather as a collective embodied phenomenon. In this cycling experience, participants are invited to pedal through the polluted area with their own bikes, collectively ‘weathering’ under the guidance of the artist. A bike is not merely a medium here; it is a speculative tool that fosters the collective embodiment of the weather. By prompting reflection on how our bodies can serve as sensing technology, the participants engage in a communal artistic practice that intersects with the environment, socio-political climates, and the human body.
